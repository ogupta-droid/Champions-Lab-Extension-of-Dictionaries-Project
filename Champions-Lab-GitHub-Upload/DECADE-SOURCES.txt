Editorial decade collection — 2026-10-31
Champions Lab editorial selections, not official UEFA decade awards or a statistical ranking. One European Cup / Champions League player per calendar decade, balancing peak performances, influence and European success. Seasons are assigned by their final year. The 1950s begin with the competition’s first final in 1956; the 2020s pick is provisional through this snapshot. The record under each pick supports the rationale, not an objective claim that alternatives are inferior. Career totals and other competitions do not determine these selections.

1950s — alfredo-di-stefano
Our pick for his defining role in the competition’s first dynasty: four winning finals before the decade ended.
4 European Cups in this decade · 1956, 1957, 1958, 1959
https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/0253-0d7f4e11f0ad-0976d26c2c98-1000--lucky-charms-top-european-cup-winners/

1960s — eusebio
Our pick for a decade-defining final performance and the attacking identity he gave Benfica.
2 goals in the 1962 final · Benfica 5–3 Real Madrid
https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/0258-0e6a045944ea-75ee25be6a88-1000--1961-62-eusebio-strikes-gold-for-benfica/

1970s — johan-cruyff
Our pick for the influence of Total Football and three successive Ajax triumphs, including his two-goal 1972 final.
3 European Cups in this decade · 1971, 1972, 1973
https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/022d-0e90fc10974b-cb5bd86b8f7d-1000--who-has-won-as-player-and-coach/

1980s — marco-van-basten
A peak-performance pick: his finishing in the 1989 final made him the attacking face of Milan’s rise. This is not a decade title-count award.
2 goals in the 1989 final · Milan 4–0 Steaua
https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/match/924/

1990s — paolo-maldini
Our defensive pick for elite consistency across Milan’s European eras and two titles during the 1990s.
2 European Cups in this decade · 1990 and 1994
https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/018f-0e6a5e8eb098-7dcc3c71f617-1000--two-more-years-for-maldini/

2000s — messi
A peak-performance pick for his 2009 campaign: the competition’s leading scorer and a goalscorer in the final. Later achievements are excluded from this rationale.
9 goals in 2008/09 · top scorer and champion
https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/01d9-0e723fa0bf50-a11f10e8b995-1000--messi-sweeps-up-goalscoring-honours/

2010s — ronaldo
Our pick for sustained knockout impact and four Real Madrid titles within the decade.
4 final wins in this decade · 2014, 2016, 2017, 2018
https://www.uefa.com/news/0271-143eb8578b52-ef62f1a96d02-1000--most-champions-league-titles/

2020s — benzema
Provisional pick for the exceptional peak of his 2021/22 title-winning campaign, when UEFA named him Player of the Season. The decade is unfinished.
15 goals in 2021/22 · UEFA Player of the Season
https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/0275-15465014cfaf-b4d5f6398256-1000/
