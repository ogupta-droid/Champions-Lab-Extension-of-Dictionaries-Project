CHAMPIONS LEAGUE LAB — HISTORY ARCHIVE
Checked 30 October 2026

71 finals from 1956–2026; 24 champion clubs; 34 stadium records.
Results cross-checked against RSSSF and the UEFA final highlights archive.
Each final links to its historical match record, containing lineups and references.
All 71 winner starting XIs and managers are included. Source-listed historical substitutes can be incomplete (especially before 1987); no missing names were fabricated.
1974 shows the replay winning XI.
Formations are display arrangements calculated from listed player positions, not claims about a fixed in-match system.
Stadium capacities are source snapshots and may differ from final-day capacity/attendance. Old and new Wembley are separate; renamed/rebuilt venues otherwise retain source identity.
Images are remote reference photographs; source and licensing pages are attached to venue records.
Player selection data is a historical final fact, not a full career-stat database.
